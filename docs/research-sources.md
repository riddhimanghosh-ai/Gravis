## RELEX Solutions

- Demand forecasting guide: [https://www.relexsolutions.com/resources/demand-forecasting/](https://www.relexsolutions.com/resources/demand-forecasting/)
  - Highlights product-location-day forecasting, flexible pooling across products and planning horizons, omnichannel needs, and the importance of external data such as weather and events.
- Measuring forecast accuracy: [https://www.relexsolutions.com/resources/measuring-forecast-accuracy/](https://www.relexsolutions.com/resources/measuring-forecast-accuracy/)
  - Emphasizes ML-based capture of demand patterns, business decisions, external factors, and explainability.
- Cannibalization and halo effects in demand forecasts: [https://www.relexsolutions.com/resources/cannibalization-halo-effects-in-demand-forecasts/](https://www.relexsolutions.com/resources/cannibalization-halo-effects-in-demand-forecasts/)
  - Useful for promotion-linked substitution and cross-SKU interactions.
- Demand sensing: [https://www.relexsolutions.com/resources/demand-sensing/](https://www.relexsolutions.com/resources/demand-sensing/)
